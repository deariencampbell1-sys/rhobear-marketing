WHAT EVERY PLAN INCLUDES
CREDITS

Model credits, not a token plan

Every plan includes credits through our own Pi routes, with a hard monthly cap so the bill never surprises you. The app shows the balance in credits and tokens. It never shows a dollar balance we can quietly move.

THE REMOTE

The app and rho

Every session in your Space, by name, live, on iPhone, iPad, Android and Mac. Type into the same thread, approve, stop this turn, end the session. rho gives you the same thing in a terminal on Mac and Linux.

THE WORK

Saved to your GitHub

Checkpoints commit and push to a rhobear/<session> branch in your repo, never to your default branch and never force-pushed. Work outlives the Space, because it was never ours to keep.

SAID PLAINLY

What Starter cannot do.

A Telegram bot that answers all day needs a service that stays up. SSH needs a port. A 40 GB disk needs a disk. Starter has none of those, and the app says so before you hit the wall.

  • No always-on bots, schedulers, mail or Telegram services.
  • No SSH in or out.
  • No extra disk volume. The work lives in GitHub instead.

Your agent knows which plan it is on, because the capability card in its context is generated from the real configuration. If a request needs something Starter does not have, it does the version that works on Starter first, and mentions the upgrade once, plainly, after the work is done. It never stalls to sell you something.

WHEN YOU MOVE UP

Prorated, both ways.

Starter to Own Space provisions your server, moves the workbench across, then retires the container. GitHub is already the home of the work, so the move is short. Going the other way is prorated too.

  • Provisioning is automatic, with real progress in the app, not an animated bar.
  • Cancel pushes your work to GitHub, keeps the Space for a stated grace period, then deletes the server and verifies it is gone.
  • Your harness sign-ins and your own keys are deleted with the Space. Your repos are yours and stay where they are.
THE BOUNDARY

A container, not a filter.

Tenants are isolated by the machine, not by a query. One container per subscriber with a hard cap on CPU, memory, processes and disk. The connector that lists your sessions runs inside your container, so it can only ever see your work.

Your harness logins and your own API keys stay inside your Space. They never transit our app, our cloud service or our logs. Our admin access is break-glass only, logged, and stated in the Terms.

THE RULES WE KEEP
  • No selling below cost, and no fake discounts on the way in.
  • No usage metering of your own keys or your own harness accounts.
  • No mining, spam, scanning or open relays on our network. That is the Acceptable use policy, and it applies to every Space.
  • Customer servers stay in a separate hosting project from ours, so one tenant's abuse can never suspend anyone else's workspace.
