HOW IT IS WIRED

A token for your account, not a key for a provider.

  • Your Space gets a scoped token to our cloud proxy. The MCP never hands you a provider key, and it never holds one.
  • Clients authenticate with your account token. No token, no tools, and the refusal is explicit rather than a timeout.
  • Every call is metered against your plan's credits, the same balance Pi draws on. There is no second invoice.
  • Your own API keys and your own harness sign-ins are never metered by us, because they never pass through us.
  • Requests are checked against the tool's purpose before they run. Something outside it is refused, not silently attempted.
PLAINLY

Not another subscription on top.

The MCP is included with Starter and Own Space. It draws on the credits already in your plan, so a diagram or a 3D draft is a line on a balance you can see, not a surprise at the end of the month.

On Own it, the MCP is a subscription add-on. The tools need an account and an account needs servers to answer from. We would rather say that than pretend the free path covers it.
