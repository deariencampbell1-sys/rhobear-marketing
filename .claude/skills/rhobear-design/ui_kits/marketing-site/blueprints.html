BLUEPRINTS · A FEATURE, NOT AN APP

Draw the plan before you spend the money.

Blueprints is not a separate app any more. It is a feature of our harness and a tool set on the RHOBEAR MCP. Ask for it in any chat thread and it draws your architecture, proposes the team with a real cost, and then waits for your yes before a single lane starts.

Where it lives →Which plan includes it →
One product, one subscription.

There is no second thing to buy and no second bill to watch. Blueprints draws on the credits already in your plan. If you are on Starter or Own Space, it is already in the harness.

THE SIX STEPS

Read, draw, propose, ask, run, report.

01
Read-only first

It gathers the thread, the repo, the notes or the email you point at, and does web research when the answer is not in front of it. Reading changes nothing.

02
Draw the plan

A colourful flowchart in the orientation you ask for, left to right or swimlanes. The flow has to make sense, not just stack boxes in a neat column.

03
Propose the team

As many lanes as the work needs, each with exact file paths, its own worktree, and a model chosen for cost: fast models for build lanes, a vision model for UX, a frontier model where architecture is being decided. One integrator stays on. The estimated cost is on the card.

04
Ask for the yes

An approval card in the app: here is the order, here is the team, here is the bill. Nothing launches without a yes. A plan is not a purchase order.

05
Run the lanes

Lanes work in parallel. Each finished lane goes through the review cycle you already use, whatever that is. Findings get fixed and re-reviewed until clean.

06
The integrator stays on

It merges, watches for conflicts, and tells you where every lane is: running, back, verifying, done. You never have to ask "is it still going".

IN THE APP

The diagram opens where you can use it.

01 · OPEN IT

A real viewer

The diagram opens in the app's own viewer. Pan, zoom, drag, reorient, tap a colour to change it. It does not need a browser tab or a third-party service.

02 · KEEP IT

Saved with the work

Save it, share it, or send it to your repo with the rest of the work. The plan lives next to the code it describes.

03 · WATCH IT

Lane status, live

Each lane reports as it moves. The integrator's report is the same one a coordinator reads, in plain language, not a spinner.

WHY IT EXISTS

Teams of agents fail in the planning, not the typing.

Five agents working from a vague brief produce five different products. The fix is not a bigger model, it is an order of work: who owns which file, which lane waits for which, and what "done" means before anyone starts.

Blueprints is that order of work, drawn where you can argue with it before it costs anything.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
  • It does not launch anything without an approval.
  • It does not invent a cost, a path or a lane count.
  • It does not require our reviewer. Bring the review cycle you already trust.
  • It does not replace your judgement. It gives you something concrete to judge.
