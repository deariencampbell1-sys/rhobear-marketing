WHAT A SPACE IS

A computer that is yours, with your agents on it.

01 · THE MACHINE

Your own Linux box

A Space is an isolated Linux machine with fixed CPU, memory and disk. Starter is a locked-down container on a shared RHOBEAR server. Own Space is a whole small server of your own. One tenant can never read, slow or crash another.

02 · THE AGENTS

Your accounts, not ours

We install the harnesses you pick and nothing else. Each one signs in through its own sign-in, with your account. Your logins and your own API keys stay inside your Space and never pass through our app, our cloud or our logs.

03 · THE REMOTE

Every session, by name

Three Codex threads are three named rows, not one frozen process. Open a session and you are in the same thread it is in: type, approve, stop this turn, end the session. History stays when a session ends.

THE REMOTE CONTROL

The buttons do exactly what they say.

No guessing which process you just killed. The app never offers to end something it cannot name, and it never invents progress: every status you see comes from a real event in your Space.

WORK STAYS YOURS

Saved to your own GitHub.

Your work belongs in your repos, not in storage we rent you. At every checkpoint the Space commits and pushes to a rhobear/<session> branch in your repo, never to your default branch, and never force-pushed. A secret scan runs first: a key, an .env file or a harness login folder blocks the push and tells you.

The Space's disk is a workbench, not an archive. Checkouts, caches and node_modules live there and are rebuilt. The code lives on GitHub, where you already keep it. If you cancel, your work is still yours; the workbench disk is deleted after the stated grace period, and we tell you before it happens.
