WHAT YOU NEED

Three things, all of them yours.

  • A RHOBEAR account. The MCP needs one, and the app pairs to your Space with it.
  • GitHub. Your work has to land somewhere you own, so a Space saves to your repos, on a branch, never force-pushed.
  • A Space. Starter on our shared server, Own Space on your own server, or Own it on your own computer.

You never need our keys. Each harness signs in with your own account, inside your Space, through its own flow.
