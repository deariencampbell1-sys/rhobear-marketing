Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · covers RHOBEAR Spaces, the RHOBEAR app, rho, the RHOBEAR MCP and this website

This policy describes how Sun Sponge LLC ("RHOBEAR", "we", "us") handles information when you use our services. If you do not agree with it, do not use the services.

1. The short version

  • We collect what we need to run your account, your plan and your Space: your email, your billing events, your GitHub access, your credit usage and the metadata that makes a session list work.
  • Your harness sign-ins and your own API keys live inside your Space and never pass through our app, our cloud service or our logs.
  • Your work is saved to your own GitHub. We do not treat it as ours.
  • We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not train models on it.
  • Our admin access to a Space is break-glass only, and logged.

The boundary is the container. Each Space is an isolated Linux machine. The connector that lists your sessions runs inside your Space, so it can only ever see that Space's work. Isolation is physical, not a query filter.

2. What we collect

a. Account information

Your email address, how you sign in, and the plan you are on. If you subscribe through the App Store, Apple tells us that a subscription is active and which product it is; we do not receive your card details.

b. Billing and plan events

Purchases, renewals, upgrades, downgrades and proration events, so the plan you see and the plan you are charged for are the same thing.

c. GitHub access

When you connect GitHub, we receive the installation and the repositories you grant us. Work is saved to branches in those repositories. We do not read repositories you have not granted.

d. Metering

Credits and tokens used against your allowance, per Space, and which model route served a request. Metering is what makes a hard monthly cap possible; it is a count, not your prompts.

e. Session metadata

For the session list to work we hold the harness name, the thread title, the project folder, the model, the status and the last activity time. This is the label on the box. The conversation itself stays in your Space.

f. Operational logs

Connection and error logs for the relay between your app and your Space, kept to keep the service running and to investigate abuse or faults. These logs do not contain your harness credentials or your own API keys.

3. What stays inside your Space

  • Harness sign-ins: your Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw, Hermes or Pi login material.
  • Any API keys you add yourself. Provider usage on your own keys is billed to you by that provider and is never metered by us.
  • Session transcripts, checkouts, caches and scratch files on the workbench disk.

These live in an encrypted per-Space folder on our volume. They are not sent to our app, our cloud service or our logs. The relay between your app and your Space carries traffic; it is a pipe, not an archive.

4. Your work, and GitHub

Your work belongs in your repositories. At each checkpoint the connector commits and pushes to a rhobear/<session> branch in the repository you granted, never to your default branch, and never by force. A secret scan runs before every push: a key, an .env file or a harness login folder blocks the push and tells you.

Files that GitHub handles badly (build output, caches, anything over 100 MB) stay on the workbench disk and are rebuilt, not committed.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal information for money or for advertising.
  • We do not train models on your content.
  • We do not route plan traffic through a reseller. Included credits go to the model providers directly (DeepInfra, Z.ai, OpenAI). OpenRouter is not used for plan traffic.
  • We do not read the contents of your Space except under the break-glass conditions in section 7.

6. Who we share it with

Only the services that make the product work, and only what each one needs:

  • Hetzner: the cloud servers your Space runs on.
  • Apple: App Store subscriptions and receipts, when you buy there.
  • GitHub: repository access you grant, so work can be saved to your repos.
  • Model providers: DeepInfra, Z.ai and OpenAI, for included credits and the requests they serve.
  • Our payment processor: if you buy on the web, it handles the card. We never see the card number.
  • Legal and safety: where the law requires it, or to stop abuse that threatens the service or other people.

Customer Spaces run in a separate hosting project from our own production services, so one tenant's conduct cannot take another tenant's workspace down.

7. Admin access and break-glass

We do not browse customer Spaces. We enter a Space only when it is necessary: to fix a fault you asked us to fix, to respond to a security incident, or where the law requires it. Break-glass access is logged, and the Terms say so, because you are trusting us with a computer.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Account and billing records stay while your account is active and afterwards as long as the law requires.
  • Metering records are kept for billing and dispute resolution.
  • When you cancel, your work is pushed to GitHub, the Space is kept for a stated grace period, then the server and its volumes are deleted and the deletion is verified. You are told before it happens.
  • Harness logins and your own keys are deleted with the Space. Your repositories are yours and stay where they are.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and personal information at any time. We will, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention.

9. Security

Credentials and tokens are encrypted at rest. Tenants are separated by container and hard resource caps. Access to production is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you if a breach affects your information.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal information, and to object to certain processing. We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of there. Contact us using the details below to exercise a right.

11. Children

The services are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International users

We operate in the United States and our servers are in the European Union (Nuremberg) and the United States. If you use the services from elsewhere, your information may be processed in those places.

13. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you before it takes effect, in the app or by email. Continuing to use the services after that means you accept the change.
