Acceptable use policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · applies to every Space, every plan and every use of the RHOBEAR MCP

You get a computer on our network. That is a lot of trust in both directions, so here is the plain version of what it may and may not be used for. This policy is part of the Terms of Service.

1. What this covers

Your Space, anything running inside it (including services your agents schedule or keep alive), the models and credits we serve, the RHOBEAR MCP, and any traffic that leaves your Space through our network.

2. Not allowed

Do not use a Space, our credits or our network to do any of these:

  • Spam. Unsolicited bulk messages by email, Telegram or any other channel, including "warm-up", list scraping and mass outreach tools.
  • Mining. Cryptocurrency mining, pool mining, or any proof-of-work workload, including a small test.
  • Scanning and attacks. Port or vulnerability scanning of systems you do not own, credential stuffing, brute force, denial of service, or exploiting a system you are not authorised to test.
  • Open relays and proxies. An open mail relay, an open proxy, a Tor exit node, or any service that lets strangers route traffic through our network.
  • Malware. Writing, hosting or distributing viruses, ransomware, stalkerware, rootkits or command-and-control infrastructure.
  • Illegal content. Anything unlawful, including material that sexualises minors. There is no warning for that one: the account ends.
  • Harm to people. Harassment, threats, doxxing, or targeted abuse of any person.
  • Infringement. Distributing content you do not have the right to distribute.
  • Breaking isolation. Attempting to reach another tenant's Space, another customer's data, or our production systems beyond the interfaces we publish.
  • Evading metering. Trying to get around the credit cap, resell included credits, or share an account to multiply an allowance.
  • Reselling the Space. Selling access to your Space or your included credits as if it were your own service.

Your agents act for you. If you put an always-on service in an Own Space, or schedule a job, what it does on our network is your responsibility. That includes the messages it sends and the endpoints it calls.

3. Secrets and other people's data

Keep your own keys inside your Space. Do not put another person's credentials in a Space you control, and do not use the workbench as a place to keep customer secrets you are not allowed to hold. A secret scan runs before every push to GitHub; it exists to stop accidents, not to give you a safe place to store keys.

4. Reporting abuse

If something coming out of a RHOBEAR Space reaches you, tell us at dearien@rhobear.ai with what you saw and when. We answer abuse reports within 24 hours. We can stop one customer's Space without touching anyone else's, and we do not need their permission to stop an attack.

5. Reporting a security problem

If you find a vulnerability in our service, tell us privately at the same address before you tell anyone else, and give us a reasonable chance to fix it. Do not test it against other customers' Spaces, and do not take data that is not yours. We will not pursue a researcher who acts in good faith under this section.

6. How we enforce this

  • Usually: we tell you what is wrong and give you a chance to fix it.
  • Sometimes: we throttle the Space or pause the offending service while we talk to you.
  • Immediately: for attacks, mining, open relays, or content that harms a person, we stop the Space first and explain afterwards.
  • At the end: repeated or serious breaches end the account. Where it is safe and legal to do so, we push your work to GitHub and give you a chance to export before deletion.

If you think we got it wrong, reply to the notice or write to the address below. A person reads it.
