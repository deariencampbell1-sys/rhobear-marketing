CONNECT RHOBEAR

Three steps,
then just ask.

  1. Add the address. In your AI app, add a custom MCP connector and paste https://api.rhobear.ai/mcp.
  2. Sign in and allow. The RHOBEAR sign-in opens. Allow the app once; it acts as you, on your account, and you can remove it any time.
  3. Ask by name. “@forge, make a wall mount for this board.” Your app sees each tool, whether it is on for your plan, and its price before it runs.
https://api.rhobear.ai/mcp

Works with ChatGPT and any app that speaks MCP over OAuth. Needs an active RHOBEAR plan.
