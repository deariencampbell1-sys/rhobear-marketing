RHOBEAR MCP · Designs

Shape it, and keep it clean.

Designs keeps the site you built true to its own design. Through the RHOBEAR connector your AI app can check an install against your design's never-list and propose a clean-up of your styles from your design's own tokens. Nothing is rewritten until you approve it. Imagine, our picture maker, comes with it.

How it works

  1. Ask in your AI app. “@designs, can I add this package?” or “@designs, tidy up the styles.” Your app calls the Designs tools through the connector, signed in as you.
  2. It checks your rules. An install command is checked against the never-list recorded for your design. A blocked install names the rule that stopped it, and the refusal is kept.
  3. You approve the change. A clean-up comes back as a proposal: the classes it would remove and the lines it would change. Apply it, and the sources and tokens are checked again first, or discard it.

The Designs tools

Your AI app sees each tool and its price before it runs. Every run draws from your RHOBEAR plan, and a run that never ran costs nothing.

  • designs.guard-checkCheck an install command against your design's never-list; a refused install is recorded.
  • designs.guard-cleanPropose a Tailwind clean-up from your locked HTML and your design's own tokens. Your sources are not rewritten.
  • designs.guard-applyApply a proposal after its source and token hashes are checked again.
  • designs.guard-discardDecline a proposal. Nothing is written.
  • image.generateImagine: three pictures from your words, returned as links. Runs in the testing grounds
  • image.editImagine: three new versions of a picture you made earlier.

In the testing grounds you can make pictures with Imagine. The Designs guard works on your own design jobs, so it runs from your AI app.
