Describe a world.
Then play it.

Connect RHOBEAR World Builder to ChatGPT or Claude. Build a floating arena, lay out a parkour course, or put a village on a ridge. Preview it, give your assistant another direction, then open the world in your browser.

Make your first world

  1. Connect your assistant. Add https://games.rhobear.ai/mcp and sign in with your RHOBEAR Games account.
  2. Describe what players should find. Pick a landscape, build with blocks and prefabs, place characters and items, and choose how players win.
  3. Preview, then play. Look at the rendered world, ask for changes, and follow the play link. Every saved world appears in World Builder.

A free Games account keeps three worlds. Building with AI comes with the RHOBEAR season pass. Your saves start private. Public sharing requires review. ChatGPT building requires a workspace with custom MCP write access. Claude supports custom connectors on its Free plan.
