RHOBEAR MCP · Search

Find it, with the source.

Search gives your AI app ranked passages from your decisions, handoffs, code, GitHub and the web, each with its source attached. Ranking orders results by how well they match; it does not prove a claim is true, and Search says so.

How it works

  1. Ask a question. “@search, where did we decide the refund rule?” Your app sends the question through the connector.
  2. Get ranked passages. Each passage carries its source, so your assistant can show where an answer came from.
  3. Ask why, or read more. Ask why one result ranked where it did, or fetch one bounded excerpt of one source from your own account.

The Search tools

Your AI app sees each tool and its price before it runs. Every run draws from your RHOBEAR plan, and a run that fails is refunded.

  • search.retrieveRanked passages from decisions, handoffs, code, GitHub and the web, each with its source.
  • search.explainWhy one result ranked where it did: its score parts and the source revision behind them.
  • search.fetchOne bounded excerpt of one source, read from your own account.

Search reads your own sources, so it runs from your AI app rather than as a sample. The testing grounds lists its tools and their prices.
