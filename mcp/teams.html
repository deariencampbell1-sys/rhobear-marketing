RHOBEAR MCP · Teams

Plan it as a team.

Teams turns a build into a Blueprint: steps and lanes you can see as a map, approve, and run. Your AI app plans from the sources you point at, draws the plan, proposes the team, and launches only the lanes you approved.

How it works

  1. Plan from your sources. Point at a thread, a repo, a file or a note. The plan is a draft with open questions, and planning never touches your repo or Space.
  2. See the map. The plan is drawn as a diagram whose boxes are the plan's own steps and lanes.
  3. Approve, then run. Each lane is priced before you approve it in the app. Only approved lanes launch, into your Space; cancelling stops new launches at the next safe point.

The Teams tools

Your AI app sees each tool and its price before it runs. Every run draws from your RHOBEAR plan, and a run that fails is refunded.

  • blueprints.planGather the sources you point at into a draft plan, with open questions. Read-only. Runs in the testing grounds
  • blueprints.diagramCheck and draw the plan's map from Mermaid; only the plan's own steps and lanes are allowed. Runs in the testing grounds
  • diagram.renderDraw any Mermaid or Excalidraw diagram as a picture. Runs in the testing grounds
  • blueprints.lanesPropose the team: check the lanes and price each one for your approval.
  • blueprints.dispatchLaunch the lanes you approved into your Space.
  • blueprints.statusEach lane's state, from your Space.
  • teams.editChange a Blueprint's title, steps, lanes or layout; it then needs your approval again.
  • teams.runRun approved lanes; cancelling stops further launches at the next safe point.

In the testing grounds you can plan a build from a note and draw its map.
