Tools · Forge

Forge has its own app

Open, fix and split 3D models for your printers. Any compatible AI app or agent (remote MCP over HTTP with OAuth sign-in) can drive Forge through @forge; the app is where you see the model and take over. The app is a separate membership.

Get the Forge appUse @forge in a thread

Add them once

  1. Add the address. In an app that connects to remote MCP servers over HTTP with OAuth sign-in (Claude Code and Codex do), add a custom MCP server: https://api.rhobear.ai/mcp
  2. Sign in and allow. The RHOBEAR sign-in opens once; the app acts as you, on your account, and you can remove it any time.
  3. Ask by name. “@forge, make a wall mount for this board.” Your app sees each tool and its price before it runs.
